## 2. Team Requirements

Our team is responsible for the **Level Design System** of the Space Invaders game.

We will design the overall stage progression of the game, starting with a tutorial that teaches players the basic controls, how to defeat enemies, and how to clear stages.

After the tutorial, players will progress from Stage 1 to Stage 10, with the difficulty gradually increasing.

Stage 5 will include a special mid-boss, and Stage 10 will include the final boss.

After clearing Stage 10, an **Endless Mode** will be unlocked. In Endless Mode, the player's goal is to survive as long as possible and achieve a high score based on survival time and the number of enemies defeated.

The difficulty of Endless Mode will gradually increase over time. Bosses will appear after a certain amount of time or after the player defeats a certain number of enemies. Defeating these bosses will provide benefits such as increased damage, increased movement speed, or other player upgrades.

## 3. Detailed Requirements

### 1. Tutorial Stage
The tutorial stage will teach players the basic controls, how to attack and defeat enemies, and how to clear a stage.

It will also explain important gameplay information, such as the fact that a boss may appear at the end of a stage.

The purpose of the tutorial is to help players understand the overall flow of the game before starting the main stages.

### 2. Mid-Boss and Final Boss
Stage 5 will include a special mid-boss, and Stage 10 will include the final boss.

These bosses will be stronger than normal bosses and will use more difficult attack patterns.

This will make the game more challenging and prevent the gameplay from becoming repetitive.

### 3. Endless Mode
After the player clears Stage 10, a special stage called **Endless Mode** will be unlocked.

Unlike the normal stages, where the goal is to clear each stage, the main goal of Endless Mode is to survive for as long as possible.

The player's score will increase based on survival time and the number of enemies defeated.

### 4. Progressive Difficulty System
The difficulty of the game will gradually increase from Stage 1 to Stage 10.

As the stage number increases, enemies may become stronger, faster, or more numerous.

The purpose of this system is to provide a smooth difficulty progression so that players can gradually improve their skills while continuing to face new challenges.

### 5. Endless Mode Boss and Reward System
In Endless Mode, the difficulty will continue to increase based on survival time.

After the player survives for a certain amount of time or defeats a certain number of enemies, a boss will appear.

When the player defeats the boss, the player will receive a gameplay benefit such as:

- Increased basic attack damage
- Increased movement speed
- Increased number of projectiles
- Other combat-related upgrades

These rewards will help the player survive longer while the difficulty continues to increase.

## 4. Dependencies on Other Teams

### 1. Player & Enemy Ship Variety
Our level design may require different types of enemies and bosses with different abilities, health, movement patterns, and attack patterns.

Therefore, we may depend on the team responsible for **Player & Enemy Ship Variety**.

### 2. Gameplay HUD
The tutorial, stage number, survival time, score, and other gameplay information need to be displayed to the player.

Therefore, we may depend on the **Gameplay HUD** team.

### 3. Records & Achievements System
Endless Mode uses survival time, number of enemies defeated, and high scores as important goals.

Therefore, we may depend on the **Records & Achievements System** team to save and display player records and high scores.
